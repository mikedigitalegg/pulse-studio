Pulse Studio - quick start for Windows
======================================

You need:
  - Ableton Live 11 or 12 (any edition)
  - Python 3.10 or newer: https://www.python.org/downloads/
    In the installer, tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
  - For the AI generators: an OpenAI or Anthropic API key, or a local model (Ollama / LM Studio).

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\pulse-studio.
2. Double-click Setup.bat. It installs everything and adds the PulseBridge
   Remote Script to Ableton Live.
3. Open (or restart) Ableton Live. Go to Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI and pick
   PulseBridge in an empty Control Surface slot (leave Input/Output as None).
4. Double-click Start.bat. Your browser opens Pulse Studio at http://127.0.0.1:8005/
   Keep the black window open while you use it.
5. In the app, go to System > AI Provider, pick a provider, paste your key and press Test.
   Then go to Styles and press "Rebuild browser index" so Pulse knows your Live library.

If Windows SmartScreen warns about the .bat files, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
Full instructions and troubleshooting are in README.md.
