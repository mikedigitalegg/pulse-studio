Samples

Send To

Where a sample goes when you press Send. Audio clips go on the PS-SMP audio track (Live 12.0.5+). Simpler puts the sample on its own MIDI track with a clip that plays it. Drum Rack pad loads it onto a pad of the track you pick.

Import

Drop audio files here or record from your microphone. WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and M4A work; the page converts them to WAV before saving.

Drop samples here, or click to choose files

Your Samples

Live's Library

Samples from Live's Core Library and your User Library, from the browser index (System → rebuild it if this is empty). These load through Live's browser, so send them to a Simpler or a Drum Rack pad.
