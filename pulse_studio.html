Compose

Full Track Arranger

Generates a set of scenes (Intro, Main, Break, Drop, Outro…) into consecutive scene slots, then plays them in order.

Generate a track or re-pick to choose instruments.

Single Scene Generator

Generate one scene into a single scene slot, with the layers you tick. Good for building a loop you can extend.

Existing clips in this slot are overwritten.
Layers

Voice Generator

Make spoken vocal one-shots (text-to-speech + FX), preview them, and optionally turn them into a MIDI clip. Needs an OpenAI key.

Perform

Vox Pads

Fire the clips on your vox track: the PS-VOX track that Compose → Voice → Send to Live fills, or any track you pick. Pad 1 fires the first slot, pad 2 the next, and so on. Keys 1–8 fire the pads while this page is open.

Recorder

Record audio from your microphone and save it to the server.

Scenes

Launch a whole scene row in Live (every track's clip in that slot).

Transitions

Fade Out ramps the Pulse tracks' volume down over 4 seconds, then stops. Wash & Drop sweeps the drum and bass macros over 4 bars.

Track Knobs

The first 8 parameters of the first device on each track.
